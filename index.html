What we do

We buy houses that need work and turn them into homes families want to live in.

Bell & Oak Homes is a local home investment and renovation company in the Greater Houston area. We move quickly, we do the work right, and we care about the streets we build on.

We buy

Homes in any condition, including foreclosures, short sales, inherited houses and homes with foundation problems. We make clear offers and close on your timeline.

We renovate

Every house gets rebuilt to a standard we would live in ourselves: sound structure, honest materials and finishes that hold up.

We sell

Finished homes go back on the market ready for a family to move in, with the work done right the first time.

Selling a house?

Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026

Bell & Oak Homes, operated by Doutex Homes LLC ("Bell & Oak," "we," "us"), respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you contact us by phone, text message or this website, how we use it and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us, such as your name, phone number, email address, property address and details about a property or your situation.
  • Records of calls and text messages with us, including dates, times and message content.
  • Basic technical information your browser sends when you visit this site, such as device type and pages viewed.

How we use it

  • To respond to your questions and requests.
  • To evaluate a property and discuss or make an offer.
  • To schedule appointments and property visits.
  • To send text messages you have agreed to receive.
  • To keep business records and meet legal obligations.

How we share it

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run our business, such as our phone and text messaging provider; with professionals working on a transaction you are part of, such as a title company, attorney or lender, when needed to complete it; or when the law requires it.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All of the categories above exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

How we protect it

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect your information. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, but we limit access to the people who need it to do their work.

How long we keep it

We keep your information as long as we need it for the purposes above, including any records the law requires us to keep.

Your choices

You can stop text messages at any time by replying STOP. You can ask us to update or delete the information we hold about you by calling or texting (346) 676-6533.

Children

Our services are meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it last changed.
